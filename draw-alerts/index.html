What does the CRS cut-off mean?

When the Canadian government conducts an Express Entry draw, they set a minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score requirement. Every candidate in the pool whose score is at or above this cut-off receives an Invitation to Apply (ITA) for Permanent Residency.

These cut-offs change frequently, typically every two weeks. The required score depends entirely on the type of draw being conducted and the number of candidates the government decides to invite on that specific day.

All-program draws

Typically fall in the 479-495 range. These invite candidates from all categories based purely on score.

PNP draws

Usually 700+ because candidates have already received a 600-point provincial nomination bonus.

Category draws

Target specific occupations like Healthcare or STEM, often featuring lower cut-offs than all-program draws.
