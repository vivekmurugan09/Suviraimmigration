Australian General Skilled Migration Visas

Select the optimal visa stream based on your points, occupation list eligibility, and settlement aspirations.

Direct PR · Federal

Subclass 189

Skilled Independent Visa. Allows you to live and work permanently anywhere across Australia. No state sponsorship or employer required.

  • MLTSSL Occupation List required
  • Minimum 65 Points (85+ competitive)
  • Unrestricted freedom of residence
  • Direct citizenship pathway after 4 years
+5 Points · High Demand

Subclass 190

Skilled Nominated Visa. State nomination awards 5 bonus points. Allows permanent residence in the sponsoring state or territory.

  • State nominated (NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA)
  • 5 automatic bonus points awarded
  • Faster invitation cycles for priority skills
  • 2-year living commitment in sponsoring state
+15 Points · Regional

Subclass 491

Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa. 5-year visa for designated regional areas that transitions automatically to Permanent Residency (Subclass 191).

  • 15 bonus points awarded on nomination
  • Gold Coast, Adelaide, Perth qualify as regional
  • Lower cut-offs for engineers, IT & trades
  • Guaranteed PR pathway after 3 regional years
Crucial Foundation

Authorised Australian Skills Assessment Bodies

Before lodging an EOI, every applicant must receive a positive skills evaluation from their designated assessing authority.

ACS

Australian Computer Society for Software Engineers, Data Analysts, Cloud Architects & Cybersecurity experts.

Engineers Australia

Mechanical, Civil, Electrical & Chemical Engineers via Washington Accord or CDR (Career Episodes) report filing.

VETASSESS

Over 350 professional and managerial occupations including Marketing, HR, Finance, Logistics & Agriculture.

ANMAC & AHPRA

Dedicated fast-track assessment for Registered Nurses, Midwives, Physiotherapists & Healthcare professionals.

Targeting Australia Subclass 189, 190 or 491?

Get a complete ANZSCO occupation mapping, CDR/ACS skills assessment review, and state nomination strategy from our team.

Check Australia PR Points +91 99403 52086

You need a minimum of 65 points on the Department of Home Affairs points test to lodge an EOI. However, competitive invitations for popular occupations typically require 80 to 90 points for Subclass 189/190.

Subclass 189 is an independent PR visa allowing you to live and work permanently anywhere in Australia without nomination. Subclass 190 is state nominated (+5 points) with a 2-year state living commitment. Subclass 491 is a regional provisional visa (+15 points) leading to permanent residency (Subclass 191) after 3 years.

Competent English (IELTS 6.0 in all bands or PTE 50) is the minimum eligibility but awards 0 points. Proficient English (IELTS 7.0 / PTE 65) awards 10 points, and Superior English (IELTS 8.0 / PTE 79) awards 20 points.

ACS (IT occupations) takes 4 to 8 weeks, Engineers Australia takes 6 to 9 weeks (fast track 15 days), and VETASSESS takes 8 to 12 weeks with priority processing available.

General Skilled Migration (189, 190, 491) is strictly limited to candidates who have not yet turned 45 at the time of invitation. Candidates over 45 may explore employer-sponsored or business investment pathways.
