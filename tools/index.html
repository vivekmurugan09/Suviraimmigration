How Our 2026 Immigration Calculators & AI Preparation Tools Work

Every calculator on www.suviraimmigration.com is built directly from the latest statutory scoring grids published by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), the Australian Department of Home Affairs (SkillSelect), the German Federal Foreign Office (§ 20a AufenthG Chancenkarte), and Immigration New Zealand (SMC 6-Point Scale). All calculations run instantaneously in your browser with zero form gate required to view your score breakdown.

Do I need to submit my phone number or email before seeing my CRS or PR points score?
No. All of our PR points calculators and cost estimators display your complete score and factor-by-factor breakdown immediately on screen without requiring contact details first.
What is the difference between the Canada 67-Point FSWP Calculator and the 1,200-Point CRS Calculator?
The 67-Point Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSWP) grid is the initial pass/fail eligibility filter required to enter the Express Entry pool. Once eligible, your profile is ranked out of 1,200 points under the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) to determine whether you receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) in bi-weekly IRCC draws.
