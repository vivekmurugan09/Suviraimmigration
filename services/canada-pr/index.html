What is Canada Express Entry — and can Chennai applicants apply?

Express Entry is Canada's primary immigration management system for skilled workers. It manages three federal programs: Federal Skilled Worker (FSW), Federal Skilled Trades (FST), and Canadian Experience Class (CEC). Candidates receive a Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score. IRCC runs regular draws inviting top-scoring candidates. As of 2026, CRS cut-offs for all-program draws range from 479 to 495.

India is Canada's #1 source country for new permanent residents. Chennai applicants — particularly IT professionals, engineers, and healthcare workers — regularly qualify via Express Entry or through Ontario's Tech Draw (OINP), which adds 600 CRS points and guarantees an Invitation to Apply (ITA).

Canada PR questions from Chennai applicants

What CRS score do I need for Canada PR in 2026?

For all-program draws, CRS cut-offs range from 479-495 as of 2026. PNP draws can accept scores as low as 435-450. Provincial Nomination adds 600 CRS points guaranteeing an ITA regardless of base score.

Can I apply for Canada PR without a job offer?

Yes. A job offer is not required for Express Entry. Having one adds 50-200 CRS points depending on NOC level but it is not mandatory.

How long does Express Entry take from India?

Total 6-18 months. Profile creation to ITA: 3-6 months. ITA to PR stamp: 6-12 months. PNP processing adds 2-4 months.

What is the difference between Express Entry and PNP?

Express Entry is the federal pool where candidates compete by CRS score. PNP is province-specific selection that adds 600 CRS points guaranteeing an ITA. Suvira routes eligible Chennai profiles through OINP, SINP, AAIP or BCPNP.

Does Suvira help with provincial nominations?

Yes. Manoj N, our Canada specialist, handles OINP Tech Draw, SINP, AAIP and BCPNP applications for eligible profiles from Chennai.

What IELTS score is needed for Canada PR?

CLB 7 minimum — IELTS 6.0 in each of the 4 bands. CLB 9+ (IELTS 8.0) gives maximum language points of 136 in Express Entry.

Can my spouse work in Canada while PR is processing?

Yes. A Spousal Open Work Permit (SOWP) can be filed simultaneously with the main PR application.

What does Canada PR cost from Chennai?

Government filing fee: CAD $1,365 per adult. ECA (WES): CAD $239. IELTS: approximately INR 16,500. Medical: approximately INR 15,000. Contact Suvira for complete service and government fee breakdown.
