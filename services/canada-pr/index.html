Express Entry Streams

Choose Your Ideal Canada PR Pathway

Express Entry manages three primary federal economic programs alongside the Provincial Nominee Program (PNP). Find which stream fits your background.

Most Popular

Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)

Designed for professionals outside Canada with foreign work experience, university degrees, and strong English or French language abilities.

  • Minimum 1 year skilled experience (NOC TEER 0/1/2/3)
  • CLB 7 in IELTS or PTE Core
  • Requires 67/100 points on the Six Selection Factors

Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)

Provinces nominate candidates based on regional labor market shortages. A nomination adds 600 points, guaranteeing an Invitation to Apply (ITA).

  • Ontario Tech Draws (OINP) for Software & IT
  • Alberta Advantage (AAIP) & Saskatchewan (SINP)
  • Accepts base CRS scores as low as 380 - 430

Canadian Experience Class (CEC)

For skilled temporary foreign workers and international graduates who have completed at least 1 year of full-time skilled work inside Canada.

  • 1 year Canadian skilled work experience
  • No proof of settlement funds required
  • Lower cut-offs in dedicated CEC rounds

CRS Scoring Grid

How Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) Scores Work

Your CRS score determines your position in the Express Entry pool out of a total 1,200 points. Here is how your credentials translate to points.

Scoring Factor Max Points Key Strategy to Optimize
Age 110 Pts Peak points awarded between ages 20–29. Points gradually scale down from age 30+.
Level of Education 150 Pts Master's Degree (135 pts) or Dual Degrees gives a substantial boost over a single Bachelor's (120 pts).
Language Proficiency (IELTS/PTE) 136 Pts Achieving CLB 9+ (IELTS 8,7,7,7) unlocks maximum skill transferability bonus (+50 pts).
Foreign Work Experience 100 Pts 3 or more years of continuous skilled experience unlocks maximum experience transferability points.
Canadian Education / Job Offer 50 – 200 Pts Canadian degree adds 15–30 pts. LMIA approved job offer adds 50 (NOC 0/1/2/3) or 200 pts.
