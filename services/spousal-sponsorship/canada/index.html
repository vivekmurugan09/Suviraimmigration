🇨🇦 Canada Family Pathways

Canada Spouse, Partner & Dependent Visa Streams

Choose the right legal pathway based on your sponsor's residency status and whether your spouse is applying from India or onshore in Canada.

Outland Sponsorship

Family Class (Outland) Spousal PR

Ideal when your spouse lives in India or outside Canada
  • Processed rapidly through IRCC centralized family offices.
  • Applicant can apply for a faster Temporary Resident Visa (TRV) after AOR to visit Canada while PR processes.
  • Direct Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR) upon approval.
Inland Sponsorship

Spouse or Common-Law in Canada Class

For spouses already residing together in Canada
  • Eligible for an Immediate Spousal Open Work Permit (SOWP) once AOR is issued.
  • Work for any employer across Canada while your PR is finalized.
  • Maintained legal status throughout the entire processing period.
Work & Study Dependents

Spousal Open Work Permit (C41 / C42)

For spouses of PGWP, Skilled Workers & Master/Doctoral Students
  • Open Work Permit valid for the same duration as the principal spouse’s permit.
  • Full-time unrestricted work rights across any Canadian province.
  • Gain Canadian work experience (CEC) to boost family Express Entry CRS points.
