🇺🇸 United States Family Pathways

United States Spouse, Partner & Dependent Visa Streams

Choose the right legal pathway based on your sponsor's residency status and whether your spouse is applying from India or onshore in United States.

IR-1 / CR-1 Immigrant

USA Spouse Green Card (IR-1 & CR-1)

For legally married spouses of U.S. Citizens & Green Card holders
  • Enter the United States directly as a lawful Permanent Resident (Green Card holder).
  • IR-1 grants an unconditional 10-year Green Card for marriages over 2 years.
  • Full USCIS I-130 + National Visa Center (NVC) + Form I-864 representation.
K-1 Fiancé(e) Visa

USA K-1 Fiancé(e) Nonimmigrant Visa

For engaged partners of U.S. Citizens planning to marry in the USA
  • Travel to the United States and marry your U.S. citizen fiancé within 90 days.
  • File Form I-485 Adjustment of Status onshore to obtain your Green Card without leaving.
  • Concurrent Employment Authorization (EAD) & Advance Parole travel card filing.
H-4 & L-2 Dependents

H-4 & L-2 Dependent Visas + EAD Work Permit

For spouses and children of H-1B and L-1 visa holders
  • Fast DS-160 consular stamping and dropbox appointment coordination in India.
  • L-2S spouses receive automatic work authorization incident to status upon U.S. entry.
  • H-4 EAD work permit filing for spouses of H-1B holders with approved I-140.
