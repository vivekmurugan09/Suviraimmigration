Legal Dossier Methodology

The 4 Pillars of a Bulletproof Relationship File

Over 85% of spousal visa delays and refusals happen due to weak relationship evidence. Suvira Immigration structures your proof across the 4 universal legal pillars.

1. Financial Interdependence

Joint bank accounts, shared household expenses, money transfers, joint loans, and official insurance beneficiary designations.

2. Cohabitation & Residence

Joint residential lease agreements, utility bills in both names, shared address government IDs, and postal correspondence history.

3. Social Context & Photos

Chronological photo album with family & friends, wedding invitations, statutory declarations from relatives, and joint travel tickets.

4. Ongoing Commitment

Communication chat logs, call logs, letters of love story progression, joint future life plans, and mutual long-term commitments.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sponsoring your spouse and family members.

How long does spousal sponsorship processing take?

Processing times depend on the destination country and route. For Canada, Inland and Outland applications typically average 10 to 12 months. Australia Partner Visas (820/801) range from 6 to 15 months. UK Spouse Visas take 8 to 12 weeks with priority processing, and USA CR-1/IR-1 takes 12 to 18 months through USCIS and NVC.

Can my spouse work while the spousal visa is processing?

Yes, in most major countries! For Canada, if you apply Inland (inside Canada), your spouse can concurrently apply for an Open Work Permit (SOWP) which is issued in 3 to 4 months. For Australia, onshore applicants receive a Bridging Visa A (BVA) with full, unlimited work rights once their substantive visa expires.

What if we have been married for less than 1 year?

There is no legal minimum marriage duration. As long as your marriage is legally registered and backed by authentic proof of a genuine relationship (engagement, wedding ceremony photos, chats, calls, joint financial planning), you are fully eligible to apply immediately after receiving your registered marriage certificate.

Is there an interview for spousal sponsorship?

When an application is prepared thoroughly with an exhaustive Suvira relationship dossier, the immigration officer is typically satisfied on paper and waives the interview. If an interview is scheduled, Suvira conducts dedicated 1-on-1 mock interview preparation to ensure you both answer questions confidently.

Can we include dependent children in the same application?

Yes! Dependent biological or legally adopted children under the age of 22 can be included as accompanying dependents on the same spousal sponsorship application, receiving permanent residence or dependent visas alongside the parent.
