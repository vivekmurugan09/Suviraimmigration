Australia Spouse, Partner & Dependent Visa Streams

Choose the right legal pathway based on your sponsor's residency status and whether your spouse is applying from India or onshore in Australia.

Offshore Stream (309 / 100)

Partner (Provisional & Migrant) Subclass 309/100

Apply from India while your partner is an Australian PR or Citizen
  • Combined two-stage application lodged simultaneously on ImmiAccount.
  • Full work and study rights in Australia immediately upon Subclass 309 grant.
  • Transitions directly to Permanent Residency (Subclass 100).
Onshore Stream (820 / 801)

Onshore Partner Visa Subclass 820/801

Apply while visiting or living in Australia with your partner
  • Automatic Bridging Visa A (BVA) with full work rights when your current visa expires.
  • Immediate enrollment in Australia’s public Medicare healthcare system.
  • Seamless progression from temporary 820 to permanent 801 residency.
Fiancé Stream (Subclass 300)

Prospective Marriage Visa (Subclass 300)

For engaged couples planning to marry in Australia
  • 9 to 15-month visa allowing you to enter Australia and legally marry your fiancé.
  • Full work rights in Australia during the validity period.
  • Switch onshore to the Subclass 820/801 Partner Visa after marriage.
