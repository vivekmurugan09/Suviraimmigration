🇬🇧 United Kingdom Family Pathways

United Kingdom Spouse, Partner & Dependent Visa Streams

Choose the right legal pathway based on your sponsor's residency status and whether your spouse is applying from India or onshore in United Kingdom.

Appendix FM Spouse

UK Spouse & Civil Partner Visa

For spouses of British Citizens or UK ILR / Settled Status holders
  • Granted for 33 months (outland) or 30 months (inland), renewable toward 5-year ILR.
  • Full right to work for any UK employer or operate a business.
  • Complete guidance on Category A/B salaried income and cash savings combination.
Skilled Worker Dependent

UK Work & Health Care Dependent Visa

For partners & children of UK Skilled Worker / Global Talent holders
  • No £29,000 minimum income threshold—only standard maintenance funds required.
  • Fast-track 3-week processing with full UK work rights (except professional sport).
  • Qualifies for ILR simultaneously with the main Skilled Worker applicant.
Fiancé(e) & Unmarried

UK Fiancé(e) & Unmarried Partner Visa

For engaged couples or partners in a 2+ year relationship
  • 6-month Fiancé(e) entry visa to register your marriage in the UK.
  • Updated UKVI rules allow Unmarried Partners without strict cohabitation if relationship is 2+ years.
  • Seamless in-country switch to the 5-year Spouse route after marriage.
