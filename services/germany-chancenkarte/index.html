Germany Opportunity Visa FAQ

What is Germany Opportunity Visa 2026?

1-year job search visa for skilled professionals without a prior job offer.

Do I need a job offer?

No. You enter Germany and search for employment after arrival.

Which Indian degrees are ZAB-recognised?

Most B.Tech, M.Tech, B.E., B.Sc from IITs, NITs, and state universities.

Minimum German language required?

Zero German required. A1/A2 adds 1 point. B1+ adds 2 points.

What is the total cost?

ZAB fee €200 + consulate fee €75 + blocked account €1,027/month required. Contact Suvira for INR breakdown.

Can I bring family?

After securing employment in Germany, you can apply for a family reunification visa.
