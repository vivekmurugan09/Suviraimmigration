Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions regarding tourist, family, and business visitor visas.

How much bank balance is required for a tourist visa?

There is no fixed minimum balance set by consulates, but as a rule of thumb, you should demonstrate funds sufficient to cover your round-trip flights, accommodation, and approximately $100 to $150 USD per day of travel, plus a financial safety cushion. Typically, maintaining ₹3 to ₹5 Lakhs in savings for a 2-week trip is recommended.

What is the difference between a Canada Visitor Visa and Super Visa?

A standard Canada Visitor Visa allows multiple entries for up to 6 months per visit over a 10-year span. The Canada Super Visa is exclusively for parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and PRs, granting continuous stays of up to 5 years per entry without having to leave or renew status.

Can I apply for a tourist visa if I had a previous refusal?

Yes, absolutely! Having a previous refusal does not permanently bar you from traveling. Suvira Immigration conducts a detailed refusal analysis, addresses the specific reasons stated in your previous decision letter (such as ties or fund verification), and files a freshly reinforced application.

Do I need to attend an in-person interview for tourist visas?

For the USA (B1/B2), an in-person consular interview is mandatory for first-time applicants aged 14 to 79. For Canada, the UK, Australia, and Schengen countries, decisions are primarily paper-based via online portals and biometrics centers (VFS Global), with interviews requested only in rare circumstances.

Can I convert a tourist visa to a work or PR visa while abroad?

In most countries (such as Australia or the UK), switching from a visitor visa to a work visa onshore is restricted and requires departing. However, in Canada, policies have periodically permitted visitors with valid LMIA job offers to apply for work permits. Our immigration lawyers will guide you based on current statutory regulations.
