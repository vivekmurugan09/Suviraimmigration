Visa Standards

New Zealand Student Visa & FTS Financial Criteria

Immigration New Zealand (INZ) requires verifiable tuition payment after Approval in Principle (AIP) and NZ$ 20,000/year living funds.

INZ Financial Rules Verified

Living Costs & Funds Transfer Scheme (FTS)

Immigration New Zealand enforces strict financial verification for Indian student visa applicants:

  • Living Cost Requirement: Must demonstrate NZ$ 20,000 per year (or NZ$ 1,667/month) for living expenses on top of first-year tuition fees.
  • Pay Tuition After AIP: Unlike other countries, you pay your 1st-year university tuition fee AFTER Immigration New Zealand issues your Approval in Principle (AIP).
  • Funds Transfer Scheme (FTS): ANZ / bank FTS option allows locked living funds to be released monthly to the student's NZ bank account upon arrival.
  • 6-Month Source of Funds: Bank statements, sanctioned education loans, or fixed deposits held for 6+ months with clear ITR income trail.

Academic, Medical & PCC Documentation

Mandatory documents for lodging your online Fee-Paying Student Visa with Immigration New Zealand:

  • Unconditional Offer of Place: Issued by an NZQA-approved university or Institute of Technology (Te Pūkenga).
  • English Proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (no band < 5.5) for Bachelor's/GD, and 6.5 overall (no band < 6.0) for Master's programs.
  • INZ Panel Physician Medical & Chest X-Ray: Full eMedical examination completed at an approved clinic in Chennai.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) & SOP: Passport Seva Kendra PCC and a detailed Statement of Purpose explaining career outcomes.
