Visa Standards

UK Student Visa Criteria & 28-Day Financial Rule

UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) enforces strict point-based criteria. Our team ensures your CAS and financial files comply 100%.

UKVI Financial Standard 28-Day Rule Verified

Maintenance Funds (28-Day Rule)

UKVI requires maintenance funds to be kept for 28 consecutive days before filing:

  • Inner London: £1,334 per month for up to 9 months (£12,006 living expenses).
  • Outer London / UK Regions: £1,023 per month for up to 9 months (£9,207 living expenses).
  • Tuition Balance: Remaining 1st year tuition fees not already shown as paid on your CAS.
  • 28-Day Proof: Funds must remain in account unbroken for at least 28 consecutive days ending no more than 31 days before application.

CAS & Academic Documents

Primary documentation required for UK Student Visa approval:

  • Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS): Issued by your licensed university sponsor.
  • English Language Proficiency: IELTS Academic (6.0–6.5) or 70%+ in Class 12 English for eligible universities.
  • Tuberculosis (TB) Certificate: From an approved UKVI clinic in India.
  • Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS): Paid online to access the National Health Service (NHS).
  • Academic Transcripts & Degree Certificates: Matching the qualifications listed on your CAS.

What is a CAS letter and why is it essential?

A Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) is an electronic reference number generated by a licensed UK university through the UKVI portal. It confirms that you have an unconditional offer of a place on a course of study and details your course fees, deposit paid, and academic qualifications evaluated.

Can I study in the UK without an IELTS test?

Yes. Many leading UK universities offer IELTS waivers if you achieved 70% or higher in English in your Class 12 CBSE, ICSE, or select state board examinations, or if your previous medium of instruction was English accompanied by an internal university interview.

How does the 2-Year Graduate Route (PSW) work?

The Graduate Route enables students who have completed an eligible undergraduate or master's degree from a recognized UK higher education provider to stay and work in the UK for 2 years (3 years for doctoral graduates). It is unsponsored, meaning you do not need a job offer to apply and you can work across any industry.

What is the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)?

The Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) gives you full access to the UK's National Health Service (NHS) during your stay on a student visa. For international students, the fee is paid at the time of online visa submission and covers doctor appointments, emergency care, and hospital treatment.

When should I start applying for the UK September intake?

Ideally, you should begin your applications between November and March for the upcoming September intake. This ensures sufficient time to obtain conditional offers, fulfill language requirements, arrange the 28-day bank maintenance, and secure your CAS without last-minute visa rush.
