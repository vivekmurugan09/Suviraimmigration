Visa Essentials

Australia Student Visa (Subclass 500) Criteria

The Department of Home Affairs (DHA) mandates strict compliance with Genuine Student (GS) assessment and financial sustainability.

DHA Subclass 500 GS Compliant

Key Subclass 500 Requirements

Primary academic, financial, and health criteria required by DHA:

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE): Official CoE from a CRICOS accredited Australian course.
  • Genuine Student (GS) Assessment: Evidence of circumstances, value of course, and future career path.
  • English Proficiency: IELTS 6.0–6.5 (min 6.0 in each band) or PTE Academic 50–58.
  • Financial Proof: Annual living expenses of AUD $29,710 + 1st year tuition fees.
  • OSHC Insurance: Comprehensive medical health coverage for the full visa duration.

Work Rights & Post-Study Visa (485)

Part-time student work privileges and post-graduation work visa durations:

  • During Studies: 48 hours per fortnight permitted during semester sessions.
  • Vacation Periods: Unlimited full-time work permitted during official semester breaks.
  • Bachelor's Graduates: 2 years Post-Study Work Visa (PSWV).
  • Master's Graduates: 3 years Post-Study Work Visa (Coursework or Research).
  • Regional Study Bonus: Additional 1–2 years extension for Adelaide, Perth, Gold Coast, etc.
