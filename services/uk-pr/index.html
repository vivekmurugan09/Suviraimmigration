Statutory Requirements

The Four Pillars of UK Indefinite Leave to Remain

Before applying for permanent settlement, every applicant must meet these four mandatory statutory criteria under the UK Immigration Rules.

Continuous Residence

Must maintain continuous residence with no more than 180 days absence in any rolling 12-month period during the qualifying 3 or 5 years.

Salary Thresholds

Confirmation from your licensed sponsor that you are paid at least the standard threshold (£38,700) or applicable occupational going rate.

Life in the UK Test

Mandatory computer-based examination covering British traditions, customs, history, and laws; valid permanently once passed.

English CEFR B1

Proof of English proficiency at CEFR Level B1 through a SELT test or Ecctis-verified English-taught university degree.

The 5-Step Roadmap to UK PR & British Citizenship

Our certified immigration attorneys guide your file systematically from in-country residence to British passport.

1

Initial Entry & CoS

Secure valid sponsorship or talent endorsement, enter the UK, and commence continuous residence tracking.

2

Continuous Residence

Maintain continuous employment, ensure absence logs stay below 180 days per year, and retain P60s & payslips.

3

Life in the UK & B1

Clear the computer-based Life in the UK examination and obtain required CEFR B1 English certification.

4

ILR Lodgement

Lodge your online SET(O) or SET(M) settlement application within 28 days of your qualifying 3 or 5-year anniversary.

5

British Passport

After 12 months with ILR status, apply for Naturalisation as a British Citizen and receive your British Passport.
