Australia General Skilled Migration FAQ

What is Subclass 189?

Skilled Independent Visa. No state nomination required. Allows you to live anywhere in Australia.

What is Subclass 190?

Skilled Nominated Visa. Requires state/territory nomination (+5 points). Must live in nominating state for 2 years.

What is Subclass 491?

Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa. Requires regional nomination (+15 points). 5-year visa leading to PR (Subclass 191) after 3 years.

How long does skill assessment take?

VETASSESS takes 3-5 months, ACS 2-4 months, Engineers Australia 3-6 months depending on document readiness.

What is the minimum IELTS score?

Competent English (IELTS 6.0 in each band or PTE equivalent) is the minimum to apply, but gives 0 points. IELTS 7.0 gives 10 points. IELTS 8.0 gives 20 points.

Can I apply for Australia PR over 45 years old?

No. General Skilled Migration is only open to applicants under 45 years of age at the time of invitation.
