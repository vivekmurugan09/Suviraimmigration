US Visa & Study Pathways from Chennai (US Consulate Mount Road)

Applicants across Chennai, OMR, Guindy, and Tamil Nadu apply for US non-immigrant and academic visas through the U.S. Consulate General on Gemini Circle (Anna Salai / Mount Road), Chennai and the VAC biometric center. Our Ekkatuthangal specialists prepare complete financial and academic files for:

  • F-1 Academic Student Visa & I-20 Coordination: Fall (August/September) and Spring (January) admissions across STEM-designated Master's and Bachelor's programs, SEVIS I-901 fee payment ($350), DS-160 filing, and 1-on-1 consular mock interviews.
  • 36-Month STEM OPT Extension: 12 months initial Optional Practical Training plus a 24-month STEM extension (3 years total US work authorization) for computer science, engineering, data analytics, and quantitative business graduates.
  • B1/B2 Tourist & Business Visitor Visa: Family visits, corporate meetings, medical consultations, and conference travel with strong home-ties documentation (CA net-worth certificates, ITRs, and employer NOC).
  • IR1 / CR1 & K-1 Spousal & Family Sponsorship: Complete Form I-130 petition assembly, National Visa Center (NVC) Form I-864 Affidavit of Support, and consular interview preparation.
What financial documents are needed for a US F-1 Visa interview in Chennai?
You must show liquid funds covering at least the first year of tuition and living expenses as stated on your Form I-20—typically via savings bank statements, sanctioned education loan letters from RBI-recognized banks or NBFCs, and sponsor Income Tax Returns (ITRs) for the past 3 years.
