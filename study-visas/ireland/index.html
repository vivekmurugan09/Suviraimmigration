Visa Standards

Ireland Study Visa (D-Study) & Financial Criteria

Irish Immigration (ISD) requires minimum €6,000 fee payment before visa filing and €10,000/year living expense proof.

ISD Financial Rules Verified

Tuition Payment & €10,000 Living Funds

Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service (ISD) financial requirements for Indian students:

  • Minimum Tuition Payment: Pay at least €6,000 (or 50% of tuition if fees are under €12,000) to the university before lodging your Irish visa application.
  • Living Cost Proof (€10,000/yr): Demonstrate immediate access to at least €10,000 for first-year living expenses via 6-month bank statements, EduBond, or sanctioned education loan.
  • 6-Month Bank History & Lump-Sum Explanation: Any large credit deposited within the 6 months prior to application must be backed by documentary proof of source.
  • Private Medical Insurance: Mandatory comprehensive student health insurance policy covering your stay in Ireland.

Academic & VFS Ireland Documentation

Primary documents required for your AVATS online application and VFS submission:

  • Unconditional Offer & ETF Receipt: Official letter of acceptance from an ILEP-listed Irish institution plus electronic fee transfer receipt.
  • English Language Proficiency: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band < 6.0), PTE Academic 63+, or Duolingo 120+.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Mandatory Passport Seva Kendra PCC and gap justification certificates for any work experience.
  • Signed Visa Application Letter: Detailed study plan committing to return home upon completion of lawful study/Stamp 1G permissions.
