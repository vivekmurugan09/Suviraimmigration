Visa Standards

US F-1 Student Visa Criteria & I-20 Financial Rules

US Consulates evaluate academic intent, liquid financial coverage for 1st-year I-20 costs, and strong home-country ties under Section 214(b).

Form I-20 & SEVIS Rules Verified 2026

Liquid Funds & Sponsorship Criteria

Demonstrate readily available funds covering 1st-year tuition and living expenses stated on your Form I-20:

  • 1st-Year I-20 Coverage: Liquid savings, fixed deposits, or sanctioned education loans covering 100% of year-one COA ($35,000–$55,000 USD depending on university).
  • Sanctioned Education Loans: Official sanction letters from nationalized banks, private banks, or NBFCs/Prodigy/MPOWER are accepted for F-1 visa interviews.
  • Sponsor Affidavit (Form I-134): Parents or blood relatives acting as sponsors must provide 3 years of ITRs, CA net-worth statements, and 6-month bank statements.
  • SEVIS I-901 & DS-160 Fee: Mandatory $350 SEVIS I-901 fee receipt and $185 MRV DS-160 visa application fee paid prior to US Embassy biometrics.

Academic & US Embassy Interview Documents

Core documents required for your VAC biometrics and US Consulate F-1 visa interview in Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, or Delhi:

  • Signed Form I-20: Official Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status issued by your SEVP-certified US university.
  • English & Standardized Tests: Official IELTS (6.5+), TOEFL iBT (80–100+), Duolingo (115+), and GRE/GMAT scorecards as listed on your I-20.
  • Transcripts & Degree Certificates: Sealed university mark sheets, provisional/convocation certificates, and WES/ECE evaluations where required.
  • Section 214(b) Interview Readiness: Clear articulation of why you chose your major, university comparison, sponsor income, and post-OPT career plans.

All F-1 graduates completing a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree receive 12 months of post-completion Optional Practical Training (OPT). If your degree is on the DHS STEM-Designated Degree Program List, you qualify for an additional 24-month STEM OPT extension—giving you 36 months (3 years) of full-time US work authorization.

Many top 100 US universities have made the GRE optional or waived it for Computer Science, Data Science, and Engineering master's programs if you hold a strong undergraduate CGPA (7.5+/10). However, a competitive GRE score (310+) remains beneficial for merit scholarships and graduate assistantships.

For the Form I-20, universities require liquid financial proof equal to 1 year of total Cost of Attendance (typically $35,000 to $55,000 USD, or ₹30–₹46 Lakhs).
