Study in Canada

Direct Pathway to Permanent Residency

Study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLI) and obtain up to a 3-year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) leading directly to Express Entry PR.

  • Up to 3-Year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

  • Direct CRS score boost for Canadian education

Ivy League & High-Tech Silicon Valley
