Engineering, Tech & Management Hub

Enjoy world-renowned education at public universities with virtually zero tuition fees. High demand for engineers and 18-month post-study job seeker visa.

  • Fast-track 1-Year Masters programs

Canada

Study in Canada

Direct Pathway to Permanent Residency

Study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLI) and obtain up to a 3-year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) leading directly to Express Entry PR.

  • Up to 3-Year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)
  • Work 20+ hours/week during study terms
  • Direct CRS score boost for Canadian education

Group of Eight (Go8) & High Salaries

World-class research facilities, sunny climate, and post-study work rights (Subclass 485) offering top international career opportunities.

Ivy League & High-Tech Silicon Valley

Pinnacle of academic prestige and global corporate networking. STEM graduates qualify for a 3-year Optional Practical Training (OPT) work authorization.
