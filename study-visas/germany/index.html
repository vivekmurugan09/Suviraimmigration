Why Choose Germany

Zero Tuition, World Engineering Capital & EU Pathways

Germany is Europe's economic powerhouse, offering tuition-free higher education, modern research facilities, and a thriving job market for skilled graduates.

Free Tuition at Public Universities

German public universities are funded by the federal government. Students from anywhere in the world study without paying tuition fees, saving tens of thousands of Euros.

Engineering & Tech Hub

Home to global engineering leaders like Siemens, BMW, Bosch, SAP, and Mercedes-Benz, offering direct industry projects, internships (Praktikum), and master theses.

18-Month Job Search Visa

Upon graduation, international students receive an 18-month residence permit to find professional employment matching their qualification, paving the way to an EU Blue Card.

Schengen Visa Mobility

Your German student residence permit allows unrestricted visa-free travel across all 29 Schengen member countries for weekend trips, conferences, and leisure.

Visa Standards

Germany Student Visa Requirements & Sperrkonto

Understanding APS verification, Sperrkonto blocked account rules, and German Embassy protocols ensures a smooth visa process.

Blocked Account (Sperrkonto)

To obtain a German Student Visa, you must demonstrate living expenses:

  • Mandatory Amount: Deposit €11,208 to €11,904 in an approved blocked account provider (Expatrio, Fintiba, Coracle).
  • Monthly Payout: In Germany, the bank disburses ~€934 to €992 each month for accommodation, food, and living costs.
  • Health Insurance: Statutory German public health insurance (TK, Barmer, or DAK) proof is required for university enrollment.
  • Semester Contribution: Nominal fee of €150–€350 paid directly to the university once per semester.

APS & University Admission

Key academic criteria for German university acceptance and visa lodge:

  • APS Certificate: Mandatory authenticity verification of your Indian degree/marksheets by the German Embassy New Delhi.
  • University Admission Letter: Unconditional *Zulassungsbescheid* from an accredited German university.
  • Language Proficiency: IELTS (6.5+) or TOEFL (90+) for English-taught programs. German (A1–B1) recommended for daily living.
  • Motivational Letter & CV: European Tabular CV (Europass) and structured letter of motivation.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions: Study in Germany

Everything you need to know about German public universities and student visa processing.

Are public university tuition fees really zero for international students?

Yes. In 15 of Germany's 16 federal states, public universities do not charge any tuition fees to domestic or international students. You only pay a semester administrative fee of €150–€350, which provides you with unlimited regional public transport for the entire semester. The only exception is Baden-Württemberg, which charges non-EU students a modest €1,500/semester.

How does the German Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) work?

A blocked account holds approximately €11,208 to €11,904 in an approved German institution before visa filing. Once you register your local residence address (Anmeldung) and open a current bank account in Germany, the blocked account releases €934–€992 per month to fund your daily living expenses.

Can I work part-time while studying in Germany?

Yes. International students from non-EU nations are legally allowed to work 140 full days or 280 half days per calendar year. Common student roles include on-campus student research assistant (HiWi), working student positions (Werkstudent) at tech firms, and retail jobs earning €13 to €18 per hour.

What is the 18-month Job Search Visa after graduation?

After graduating from a recognized German university, your student visa can be extended for up to 18 months under Section 20 of the German Residence Act. This gives you ample time to search for a qualified job in your field. Once you secure an employment contract, you can directly transition to the EU Blue Card or a German work residence permit.

How long does it take to obtain an APS Certificate?

The APS process currently takes between 3 to 6 weeks from physical document receipt at the New Delhi office. Because German universities and the visa consulate require an APS certificate before proceeding, we strongly recommend initiating your APS verification 5 to 6 months before your intended intake.
