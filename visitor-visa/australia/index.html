Subclass 600 Tourist

Tourist Stream (Outside Australia)

For leisure holidays, sightseeing & visiting family/friends
  • Granted for 3, 6, or 12 months with single or multiple entries.
  • Detailed day-by-day Australian itinerary & Genuine Visitor cover letter.
