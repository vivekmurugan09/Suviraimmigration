Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

We collect your full name, phone number, email address, date of birth, educational qualifications, work experience details, and target visa country — provided by you through our contact forms and eligibility calculator.

Why we collect it

To provide immigration consultancy services, respond to your inquiries, assess your eligibility, and send relevant updates about visa pathways and Express Entry draw results when you subscribe.

Who we share it with

We use Google Analytics for anonymised website traffic data only. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Your data is retained for 2 years from your last contact with us. You may request deletion at any time by emailing info@suviraimmigration.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, email us at info@suviraimmigration.com. We comply with the India IT Act 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

Cookies

We use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
