Australia PR vs Canada PR from India: Points, Costs, Processing Time & Job Market Compared

Choosing between Canada Express Entry PR and Australia General Skilled Migration (Subclass 189, 190 & 491) is the most common strategic decision faced by IT engineers, finance professionals, healthcare workers, and manufacturing specialists visiting our Ekkatuthangal office in Chennai. While both countries grant permanent residency, universal public healthcare (Medicare in Australia / provincial OHIP & MSP in Canada), free public schooling for children, and a 3-to-4 year pathway to citizenship, their age brackets, skill assessment rules, points grids, and upfront fee structures differ substantially (Sources: IRCC Canada.ca & Australian Department of Home Affairs SkillSelect, immi.homeaffairs.gov.au, verified recently).

2. Why Age 32–39 Favors Australia PR Over Direct Canada FSWP

In Canada's CRS system, an applicant loses roughly 5.5 points every birthday after turning 30—meaning by age 37, you have lost 44 CRS points compared to a 29-year-old. In contrast, under the Australian Department of Home Affairs Points Table, applicants aged 25 to 32 score 30 points, and applicants aged 33 to 39 score 25 points—a difference of only 5 points across an entire decade! Furthermore, Australia awards 15 points for 8+ years of overseas skilled work experience (whereas Canada caps foreign work experience at 3 years).

3. Skill Assessments Explained: WES (Canada) vs. ACS, Engineers Australia & VETASSESS

  • Canada (WES / IQAS): Evaluates only your academic degree equivalency (e.g., Anna University B.E. = Canadian 4-year Bachelor's). Your work experience letters are verified later by IRCC case officers after you receive an ITA.
  • Australia (ACS / Engineers Australia / VETASSESS / ANMAC): Evaluates both your qualification and your employment reference letters upfront before you lodge your SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI):
    • Australian Computer Society (ACS): For Software Engineers (ANZSCO 261313), Developer Programmers (261312), ICT Business Analysts (261111), and Cyber Security Specialists.
    • Engineers Australia (EA): For Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, and Chemical Engineers via a Competency Demonstration Report (CDR) or Washington Accord pathway.
    • VETASSESS: For Management Consultants, Marketing Specialists, HR Advisers, Internal Auditors, and Project Administrators.

4. Understanding Australia's Three General Skilled Migration Visas (189, 190 & 491)

  1. Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent Visa): Direct permanent residency without state sponsorship. You can live and work anywhere in Australia (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth).
  2. Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated Visa — +5 Bonus Points): Direct permanent residency nominated by an Australian state or territory (NSW, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, ACT).
  3. Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional Provisional Visa — +15 Bonus Points): A 5-year regional residence visa nominated by a state for cities such as Adelaide, Perth, Gold Coast, Canberra, Wollongong, and Geelong. Living and working in a designated regional area for 3 years qualifies you for permanent residency under Subclass 191.

5. Verdict: Which Pathway Should You Choose from Chennai ?

  • Choose Canada Express Entry / PNP if: You are aged 22–33 with a Master's degree or dual credentials, score CLB 9+ in IELTS/CELPIP, work in a STEM or Healthcare NOC, or are learning French (NCLC 7+), and prefer lower upfront government visa fees.
  • Choose Australia Subclass 189 / 190 / 491 if: You are aged 30–41 with 5 to 10+ years of specialized experience in Engineering, IT, Healthcare, Construction, or Education, excel at PTE Academic (79+ Superior English = 20 points), and prefer Australia's warmer climate and closer time-zone proximity to Chennai.

Frequently Asked Questions: Australia PR vs. Canada PR

1. Can I apply for both Canada Express Entry and Australia SkillSelect at the same time?

Yes. Entering the Canada Express Entry pool and submitting an Australia SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI) are completely independent and free of government filing fees. Many Chennai applicants run both profiles concurrently and pay the final visa fee only to whichever country issues their invitation first.

2. Why do many Indian applicants score higher on PTE Academic for Australia PR?

Australia's Department of Home Affairs accepts PTE Academic for all skilled migration visas. Achieving 79+ in each band ("Superior English") awards the full 20 points on the Australia points table (compared to 10 points for Proficient English / 65+ and 0 points for Competent English / 50+).

3. What is the total cost difference between Australia PR and Canada PR for a couple?

For a married couple without children, Canada PR government and assessment fees total roughly ₹2.35 Lakhs INR (plus showing ₹11.43 Lakhs in your own savings account). Australia PR government visa fees ($4,770 + $2,385 AUD) plus skill assessment and PTE total roughly ₹4.65 to ₹5.10 Lakhs INR, though Subclass 189 requires no separate bank settlement funds audit.

4. Does ACS deduct years of work experience for non-IT Bachelor's degrees?

Yes. Under Australian Computer Society (ACS) guidelines, a B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science or IT typically has a 2-year deeming period to reach the skilled date, whereas an IT professional with a Mechanical or ECE degree may have 4 to 6 years deducted via Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) before subsequent years count for Australia points. In Canada, all skilled years count without ACS deeming deductions.

5. Are Perth and Adelaide considered regional areas for +15 points in Australia?

Yes! Under Home Affairs designated regional postcodes, every city and suburb in Australia except Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane is classified as a Designated Regional Area—meaning major metropolitan hubs like Perth, Adelaide, Canberra, and Gold Coast qualify for Subclass 491 (+15 points).

6. How long does it take to get citizenship after landing as a PR in Canada vs. Australia?

In Canada, permanent residents become eligible to apply for Canadian citizenship after physically residing in Canada for 1,095 days (3 years) within a 5-year period. In Australia, permanent residents become eligible for Australian citizenship after 4 years of lawful residence (including at least 12 months as a permanent resident).
