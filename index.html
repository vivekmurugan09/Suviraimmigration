FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our visa assessment, transparent service fees, and application filing process.

How long does the visa process take?

Processing times vary by country and visa category. Typically:

  • Overseas Student Visas: 4-8 weeks
  • Visitor & Spousal Visas: 4-12 weeks
  • Permanent Residency (PR) Applications: 6-12 months

We provide realistic, verified timelines during your initial consultation.

What documents do I need for initial evaluation?

Basic requirements include:

  • Passport (valid for 6+ months)
  • Educational marksheets & degree certificates
  • Work experience letters & payslips
  • Financial proofs & bank statements
  • Language test scores (IELTS / PTE / German B1/B2 if available)

We provide a customized document checklist tailored specifically to your profile.

What are Suvira Immigration's service fees and payment terms?

We operate with 100% transparency and zero hidden charges:

  • All consultancy & service fees are disclosed upfront in writing
  • No surprise charges or unexplained costs during processing
  • Flexible milestone-based payment structure as your application moves forward

We provide official GST invoices for all retainer agreements.

Do you assist with Spouse and Dependent Visas?

Yes, we provide end-to-end family immigration support including:

  • Spousal Sponsorship & Dependent Visas (Canada)
  • Subclass 500 / 485 / 189 / 190 Dependent Visa processing for Australia
  • Family Reunification Visas for Germany & European destinations
  • Child Student & Dependent Visas

Our experts handle simultaneous dual-applicant filings to keep families together.

What languages do Suvira consultants speak?

Our consultants are fluent in English and Tamil. Saritha V, our Germany specialist, also speaks German (B2 level). All consultations available in Tamil or English based on your preference.

How can I check my CRS or PR points eligibility for free?

You can evaluate your profile instantly using our free online points calculators for Canada Express Entry (CRS & 67-Point FSW), Australia General Skilled Migration (Subclass 189/190/491), Germany Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte), and New Zealand SMC, or book a free 1-on-1 profile audit with our senior specialists.
