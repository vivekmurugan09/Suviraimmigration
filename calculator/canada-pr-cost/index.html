Expense Schedule

When Do You Pay Each Canada PR Expense?

Expenses are distributed over the entire immigration timeline rather than paid upfront.

Stage 1: Profile Creation (Month 1-2)

Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) through WES (~₹20,000) and English test IELTS or PTE Core (~₹17,500). Creating your Express Entry and PNP profile is 100% free with the Government of Canada.

Stage 2: Post-ITA Filing (Month 3-5)

Upon receiving your Invitation to Apply (ITA), pay the IRCC processing fee ($950 CAD) and the Right of Permanent Residence Fee ($575 CAD) directly to the Canadian Government via credit card.

Stage 3: Medicals & Landing (Month 6)

Panel physician medical exams (~₹8,000/person), Police Clearance from Passport Seva Kendra (₹500), and maintaining your Proof of Funds in your bank account until COPR issuance and landing.
