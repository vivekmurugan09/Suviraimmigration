Selection Criteria

Understanding the 6 Selection Factors of FSW

To be invited under the Federal Skilled Worker Program, candidates must first satisfy the 67-point pass mark. Once in the pool, you will be ranked under the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS).

Selection Factor Maximum Points Key Requirements for Maximum Score
1. Language Skills 28 Points CLB 9 in English (IELTS L 8.0, R 7.0, W 7.0, S 7.0) provides 24 pts. A second language at CLB 5+ adds 4 bonus points.
2. Education 25 Points Ph.D. earns 25 pts. Master's degrees earn 23 pts. Two or more post-secondary credentials earn 22 pts. Bachelor's earns 21 pts.
3. Work Experience 15 Points 6 or more years of full-time skilled work in NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3 earns maximum 15 points. Minimum 1 year is mandatory.
4. Age 12 Points Applicants aged 18 to 35 receive the maximum 12 points. Points reduce by 1 point each year after age 35 until 47.
5. Arranged Employment 10 Points Full-time, permanent job offer from a Canadian employer with an approved Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA).
6. Adaptability 10 Points Points for spouse's language skills (5 pts), blood relatives in Canada (5 pts), past Canadian study or Canadian work.

FAQ Guide

Frequently Asked Questions: Canada 67 Points

The 67-point grid is a pass/fail eligibility threshold under the Federal Skilled Worker Program. You must score 67/100 just to be allowed into the Express Entry pool. Once inside the pool, you are ranked against other candidates using the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS), which is scored out of 1,200 points.
You cannot enter the Federal Skilled Worker pool with under 67 points. However, you can bridge the gap by: (1) Retaking IELTS or PTE Core to score CLB 9+ in all 4 bands (+4 pts), (2) Having your spouse take a language test (+5 pts), (3) Learning basic French for CLB 5 (+4 pts), or (4) Exploring Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs) that operate independent occupation streams.
For English, IRCC accepts IELTS General Training, CELPIP General, and PTE Core. For French, TEF Canada and TCF Canada are accepted. Academic tests (like IELTS Academic) are strictly not accepted for Canadian PR applications.
An Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) report issued by designated organizations like WES, ICAS, IQAS, or CES is valid for 5 years from the date of issue.
