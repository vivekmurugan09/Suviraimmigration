Understanding Your Score & Recent Draw Cutoffs

🍁 Canada Express Entry CRS

General CRS cutoffs range 490–530. If your score is 400–470, Suvira routes your application through Provincial Nomination (OINP, AAIP, SINP) for an instant +600 points boost.

🇦🇺 Australia GSM (189/190/491)

Minimum requirement is 65 points. Key score drivers include Age (up to 30 pts), English Superior (20 pts), Experience (15 pts), and State Nomination (+5 or +15 pts).

🇩🇪 Germany Opportunity Visa 2026

Requires 6 out of 6 points. Points awarded for recognized qualifications (4 pts), 5+ years experience (3 pts), B1/A2 German (2 pts), and age under 35 (1 pt).

🇬🇧 UK Skilled Worker

You need 70 points to apply. 50 points are mandatory (job offer, skill level, English), and 20 are tradeable (salary, shortage occupation, or PhD).
