Frequently Asked Questions: Chancenkarte

The Central Office for Foreign Education (ZAB) in Bonn issues an official Statement of Comparability that evaluates foreign degrees (e.g., from Indian universities) and certifies which German qualification level it corresponds to (e.g., German Bachelor's or Master's degree).
The Opportunity Card is initially issued to the individual applicant. Once you find a qualified job in Germany and convert your residence status to an EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker permit, your spouse and children can immediately join you with unrestricted work rights.
Munich, Berlin, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Hamburg, and Düsseldorf host Germany's highest concentration of tech companies, automotive giants, and industrial engineering enterprises actively hiring English-speaking professionals.
