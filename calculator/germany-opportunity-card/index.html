Key Entitlements

What Rights Does the Germany Opportunity Card Grant?

Designed under the 2024 Skilled Immigration Act (FEG) to enable international talent to enter Germany without an existing job offer.

20 Hours/Week Part-Time Work

Unlike old Job Seeker visas, the Opportunity Card allows you to take up secondary employment for up to 20 hours per week from day one to cover living expenses.

2-Week Trial Employment

Engage in 2-week probationary employment with prospective employers to demonstrate your technical capabilities before finalizing a full-time employment contract.

Switch to EU Blue Card / PR

Upon securing a qualifying full-time job offer in Germany, you seamlessly switch your status inside Germany to an EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker permit, leading to PR in 21–36 months.
