Payment Milestones

The 3 Spending Phases of Australia PR

You do not pay all expenses at once. Spending is phased out over 8–14 months.

Phase 1: Skills & English (Month 1-2)

Initial expenses include Skills Assessment fee with ACS / Engineers Australia / VETASSESS (~AUD 560–1,200) and English examination fee (PTE Academic / IELTS ~₹17,000).

Phase 2: EOI & State (Month 3-5)

Submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) on SkillSelect is 100% free. If applying for Subclass 190/491, state nomination application fees range from AUD $0 (VIC, WA) to AUD $330 (NSW).

Phase 3: Visa Lodgement (Month 6+)

Upon receiving your formal ITA from the Department of Home Affairs, pay the main Visa Application Charge (AUD 4,770) + spouse/dependents fee, followed by medical exams (~₹7,500/person) and PCC.

FAQ Guide

Frequently Asked Questions: Australia PR Costs

For the Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent) visa, the Department of Home Affairs does not have any statutory proof of funds requirement. For Subclass 190 and 491, certain states (like South Australia or Queensland) require demonstrating basic settlement liquidity (typically AUD $20,000 to $35,000), while Victoria and NSW do not require bank balances.
If an adult dependent (spouse) aged 18+ does not possess Functional English (IELTS overall 4.5 or PTE 30), the Department of Home Affairs charges a second instalment fee of AUD 4,885. Having your spouse score basic Functional English eliminates this charge completely.
DHA accepts international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) through ImmiAccount. Note that Australian Government merchant surcharge (around 1.4%) applies to card payments.
