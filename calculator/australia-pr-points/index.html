GSM Visa Categories

Comparing Australia PR Subclasses (189, 190, 491)

Each General Skilled Migration visa subclass has distinct nomination criteria and point requirements.

Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent)

Direct Permanent Residency from day one with zero employer or state nomination needed. Full freedom to live and work anywhere across Australia. Typically requires 85+ points for IT, Engineering, and Finance occupations.

Subclass 190 (State Nominated PR)

Permanent Residency visa granting +5 bonus points on nomination by an Australian State/Territory (NSW, Victoria, WA, QLD, SA). Requires a 2-year residency commitment to the sponsoring state. Competitive around 75-85 points.

Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional)

5-year provisional visa providing +15 bonus points for candidates nominated by regional states. Seamless transition to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 years of living and working in regional Australia.
