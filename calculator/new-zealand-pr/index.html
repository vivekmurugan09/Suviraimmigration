Statutory Framework

How the New Zealand SMC 6-Points System Works

Immigration New Zealand launched the 6-point simplified SMC framework to offer a direct, objective route to residency for skilled migrants.

Educational Route (3 to 6 Pts)

Ph.D. holders receive all 6 points immediately. Master's degrees grant 5 points (requiring only 1 year of NZ experience to reach 6). Bachelor's degrees grant 3 points (requiring 3 years of NZ experience).

Green List Pathways

Occupations on Tier 1 of the Green List (software engineers, doctors, civil engineers) qualify for Straight to Residence without having to wait for points calculation or years of work.

Income Route (3 to 6 Pts)

Earn 1.5x median wage ($47.41/hr) for 3 points, 2x median wage ($63.22/hr) for 4 points, or 3x median wage ($94.83/hr) for direct 6 points regardless of degree qualification.
